# Upstream source attribution

The field inventory includes descriptions extracted from these official MIT-licensed specifications:

- GitHub, [github/rest-api-description](https://github.com/github/rest-api-description), pinned commit and hash in `data/sources.json`. [Upstream license](upstream-licenses/GitHub-LICENSE.txt).
- Stripe, [stripe/openapi](https://github.com/stripe/openapi), pinned commit and hash in `data/sources.json`. [Upstream license](upstream-licenses/Stripe-LICENSE.txt).

Full upstream specifications are not bundled. Their exact retrieval URLs are retained and the audit verifies SHA-256 before use. Dependencies have their own licenses; the original-code MIT license does not replace them.
